## 💫 About Me

👋 Hi, I’m **Kazibwe Francis**, a versatile and growth-driven **Software Engineer** based in Kampala, Uganda, passionate about crafting innovative solutions across **Mobile**, **Web**, and **AI-powered systems**. I'm currently a **Systems Engineer at Sandi AI Technologies** and a BSc Software Engineering student at **Makerere University**. As a **Full-Stack Engineer**, I work across the stack with **TypeScript (Node.js)**, **React / React Native**, **Flutter**, **PHP (Laravel)** and **C# (.NET Core)**, and I'm now moving into **Data and AI**.

---

### 🚀 What I’m Working On

I'm currently developing and contributing to several impactful projects:

* 🏘 **Sandi AI**: A fintech platform that digitizes community savings groups (VSLAs) in Uganda. The React Native app is live on Google Play, and I work across the backend, web app and mobile app.
* 🌾 **FarmEase**: My final year project. A dairy cattle management platform with QR ear-tag registration, milk production tracking and an ML layer for disease surveillance. It grew out of the FarmEase initiative I co-founded at Ndejje University.
* 🧠 **Crest AI**: Exploring **cutting-edge AI technology** to power intelligent applications in education, finance, and agriculture.

---

### 🛠 Projects I've Built

* 🏨 **Hoscover**: A multi-tenant SaaS platform for hospitality, built for Jurya Technologies. I led the team, hardened tenant isolation and the real-time layer, built a SOAP adapter for a property management system, and shipped an AI WhatsApp concierge. [Live demo](https://hoscover-frontend-snowy.vercel.app)
* 🏫 **DHQ AI**: A school attendance system for rural schools across Uganda, built for Keti AI, with geofenced teacher check-ins and student attendance captured from a photo of the register. I built the frontend and the mobile app.
* 🌍 **PACT**: A mobile app that helps enumerators in Sudan digitize field visits to refugee areas for a WFP food program, with photo verification and delivery tracking. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pact.workflow)
* ✈️ **UCAA Survey System**: A Laravel survey platform available in English, Luganda and Kiswahili, tested with PHPUnit. [Live](https://ucaa-survey.hippozug.com)
* 🎙 **Crest AI**: A FastAPI backend using Whisper and Sunbird speech recognition for African languages.
* 🏛 **NSSF Uganda Internship**: A Laravel service helpdesk with multi-department roles, and a .NET Core backend that pulls data from two legacy systems into one analytics dashboard.
* 🏘 **Property Management System**: Led web development at Prime Link Ventures on a PMS for real estate developers.
* 👶 **AI Nanny**: A Figma and Flutter concept built for Google Hackathon 2024.
